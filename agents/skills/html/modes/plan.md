# Plan

## Source

List every item the plan states: problem and background, outcome, scope, restrictions, steps, decisions and reasons, rejected options, dependencies, owners, dates, statuses, assumptions, risks, open questions, files, commands, and completion checks.

## Fidelity

- Add no task, owner, date, estimate, status, priority, dependency, risk, or metric that the plan does not state.
- Drop nothing that affects the work.
- Leave out features and tasks that are not part of the plan, including items the plan lists as out of scope or not needed. Show something as not allowed only when the plan forbids it directly and explicitly.
- Planned work never reads as done.

## Writing

Open with one or two sentences that say what the plan delivers.

## Form

- The problem the plan solves goes in a Problem section: paragraphs for the background, and charts or diagrams when the plan gives numbers or structure.
- Ordered actions or phases use `.steps`. Each step says what it changes and, when the plan states them, its rules, dependencies, completion check, and undo.
- The parts of the plan and their results go in a summary table. Add a status column only when the plan gives statuses.
- Files to change go in a folder tree with one very short line per file. Omit it when the list does not help, such as for very large changes.
- Completion checks use `.checklist`.
- Decisions, rejected options, risks, assumptions, and open questions go in one bulleted list in the Notes section. Each item opens with the item in bold and an icon that shows its kind.
