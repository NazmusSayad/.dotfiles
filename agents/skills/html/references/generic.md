# Generic profile

Use this profile when the material is not primarily a plan, report, or explanation. Let the source's purpose and relationships determine the document rather than forcing it into a familiar format.

## Coverage

Preserve every item that affects the source's meaning or the user's stated purpose. Keep the source's hierarchy when that hierarchy carries meaning. When the source has no useful hierarchy, group closely related material and use descriptive headings.

## Writing

Honor a stated audience and level of detail. Preserve exact domain terms. Do not make the document sound like a plan, report, or tutorial unless the material has that purpose.

Do not create conventional sections such as Summary, Recommendations, or Next steps without supporting content.

## Structure

Use the fewest sections and components that make the material easy to read. Prefer prose and lists. Use tables and visualizations only when they reveal an actual comparison, sequence, or relationship.

The generic example demonstrates a neutral reference document with prose, a compact list, a table, a callout, and optional detail. These components are not required together.
