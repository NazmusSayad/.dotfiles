# Review

Review the whole edit before the final render, and again after any change. Look at frames instead of trusting the timeline: stills at every cut (the last frame out and the first frame in), each transition frame by frame, and a contact sheet of the whole video.

## Privacy

- Nothing personal or secret appears on screen: credentials, environment variables, history, private files, emails, or personal names and paths the user hasn't approved. Check text that only flashes by, too.
- Fix it at the source and record again when you can. When you can't, blur the area in Remotion on every frame where it's visible, strongly enough that nothing can be read, and follow it if it moves.

## Glitches

- Stutters, frozen, duplicated or dropped frames.
- Half-loaded or half-redrawn UI, flicker, a wrong or stale state for a frame or two.
- Black or empty frames between clips.

## Unwanted flashes

- A frame of the previous clip leaking into the next, or a flash from a load or repaint.
- Pop-ups, notifications, tooltips, a stray pointer or a selection that wasn't meant to be there.

## Mistimed cuts

- A cut that lands mid-action, starts after the moment began, or ends before the result is readable.
- Typing cut off mid-word, or a result shown before the action that causes it.
- A caption that doesn't match what's on screen, or a cut that misses its beat.

## Cropping

- Window chrome, toolbars, rounded corners or desktop showing at the edges of the footage.
- Content cut off at an edge or covered by a caption, frame or zoom.
- Padding or empty space the real product doesn't have.

## Text and audio

- Typos, and names spelled the way their owners spell them.
- No clicks at audio joins, and the music ends with the video.

After the final render, repeat the privacy and flash checks on the rendered file, because a zoom or transition can expose something the edit didn't.
