# Review

Review the whole edit before the final render, and again after any change. Look at frames instead of trusting the timeline: render stills in Remotion at every cut (the last frame of the outgoing clip and the first of the incoming one), step frame by frame through each transition, and make a contact sheet of the whole video.

## Privacy

- Nothing personal or secret appears on screen: tokens, keys, passwords, environment variables, shell history, private files, emails, or personal names and paths beyond what the user is fine with. Check text that only flashes by, too.
- Fix it at the source and re-record when you can. When you can't, blur the area in Remotion on every frame where it's visible, strongly enough that nothing can be read, and follow it if it moves.

## Glitches

- Stutters, frozen or duplicated frames, dropped frames.
- Half-loaded or half-redrawn UI, flicker, a wrong or stale state for a frame or two.
- Black or empty frames between clips.

## Unwanted flashes

- A frame of the previous clip leaking into the next, or a white flash from a page load or repaint.
- Pop-ups, notifications, tooltips, a stray cursor, or a selection highlight that wasn't meant to be there.

## Mistimed cuts

- A cut that lands mid-action, starts after the moment began, or ends before the result is readable.
- Typing cut off mid-word, or a result shown before the action that causes it.
- A caption that doesn't match what's on screen at that moment.
- A cut that misses its beat.

## Cropping

- Window chrome, toolbars, rounded corners or desktop showing at the edges of the footage.
- Product content cut off at an edge, or covered by a caption, frame or zoom.
- Gaps, padding or empty space the real product doesn't have.

## Text and audio

- Typos, product and brand names spelled as their owners spell them, captions on screen long enough to read twice.
- No clicks at audio joins; the music fades out with the video and ends with it.

After the final render, repeat the privacy and flash checks on the rendered file, because a zoom or transition can expose something the edit didn't show.
