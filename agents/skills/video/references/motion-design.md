# Motion design

## Look

- Build the palette from the product's own colors on a plain dark background.
- Avoid what reads as AI-made: green/blue/purple gradients and glows, and small "pill" badges above titles.
- Use real logos and icons wherever the product shows them. Generic placeholders look fake.
- Footage fills its frame. No empty space or padding that the real product doesn't have.

## Type

- One sans family for captions; the product's own font inside the footage.
- Short captions, one idea each ("A tab for every task." / "A group for every project.").
- Give a caption enough time on screen to be read twice.

## Motion

- Every animation is a function of the frame number (springs, eased ramps), so renders are deterministic.
- Vary transitions (whip, zoom, wipe, cut) and tie each to the rhythm: quick ones on beats, a hard cut on the big hit.
- Slow, slight camera moves (tilt, push-in) keep static footage alive. Zoom in on detail only when the viewer needs it to read something.
- Keep the overall pace relaxed and smooth. When in doubt, slow down.

## Frames

- Put footage in a simple device frame (browser window, phone) only when it helps the viewer understand where they are.
- Never draw anything that imitates the product or another product's UI.
