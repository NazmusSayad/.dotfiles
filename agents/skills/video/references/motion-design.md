# Motion design

- Every animation is a function of the frame number (springs, eased ramps), so renders are deterministic.
- Vary transitions (whip, zoom, wipe, cut), tie each to the rhythm, and match their energy to the music: with calm music, prefer soft ones and save hard cuts for the big hit.
- Slow, slight camera moves (tilt easing toward flat, push-in) keep static footage alive. Zoom in on detail only when the viewer needs it to read something.
- Give the biggest claim a hit: a hard cut, a short pause, then the line landing on a strong beat.
- Keep the overall pace relaxed and smooth. When in doubt, slow down.
