## Rules

- **Real only.** Every product visual comes from the real product with real data. Motion graphics go around the footage; never draw or simulate the product's UI. Every claim on screen is verified first.
- **1920x1080 at a true 60 fps,** unless the user asks otherwise. True means 60 distinct frames per second, measured, not 30 fps doubled.
- **Taste belongs to the user.** Show music, look and pacing choices early, before building on them.

## Order

Work through these in order. Read each file when you reach that stage.

1. **Planning:** [references/planning.md](references/planning.md). Decide who the audience is first, then the story, the scenes, the claims and the shot list, before touching footage.
2. **Editing:** [references/editing.md](references/editing.md). Turn raw takes into timed shots in Remotion.
3. **Motion design:** [references/motion-design.md](references/motion-design.md). Captions, transitions, frames and the overall look.
4. **Music:** [references/music.md](references/music.md). Pick the track, then lock the cuts to its beat.
5. **Rendering:** [references/rendering.md](references/rendering.md). Render, verify and deliver the video.

Music is listed after motion design, but choose the track early (during planning if you can): the beat grid sets the cut points for the edit.
