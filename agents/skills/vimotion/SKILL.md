## Rules

- **Real only.** Every product visual comes from the real product with real data. Motion graphics go around the footage; never draw or simulate the product's UI. Every claim on screen is verified first.
- **Raw material is never edited.** Recordings, screenshots and assets stay exactly as captured or published. Every cut, crop, blur or speed change happens in Remotion.
- **1920x1080 at a true 60 fps,** unless the user asks otherwise. True means 60 distinct frames per second, measured, not 30 fps doubled.
- **Taste belongs to the user.** Ask for their confirmation at each checkpoint before building on it: the music shortlist, one still of the look, and the full cut with its audio before the final render.

## Order

Work through these in order. Read each file when you reach that stage.

1. **Planning:** [references/planning.md](references/planning.md). Decide who the audience is first, then the story, the scenes, the claims and the shot list, before touching footage.
2. **Material:** [references/material.md](references/material.md). Gather real recordings and assets, including official logos for every brand shown.
3. **Audio:** [references/audio.md](references/audio.md). Pick the track with the user and build the beat grid the edit cuts on.
4. **Design:** [references/design.md](references/design.md). Set the palette, background, type and layouts.
5. **Editing:** [references/editing.md](references/editing.md). Check takes, then turn them into timed shots in Remotion.
6. **Motion design:** [references/motion-design.md](references/motion-design.md). Animate transitions, camera moves and the big moments.
7. **Review:** [references/review.md](references/review.md). Check the whole edit for privacy, glitches, flashes, mistimed cuts and cropping before rendering.
8. **Rendering:** [references/rendering.md](references/rendering.md). Render, verify and deliver the video.
